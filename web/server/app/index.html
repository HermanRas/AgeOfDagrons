The game

Age of Empires II, with dragons, on a phone

The strategy formula you know (villagers, resources, build queues, ages, tech trees, armies), rebuilt for a landscape touchscreen instead of a mouse and keyboard.

Four resources

Wood, food, gold and stone. Chop forests, hunt deer, farm the fields, mine gold and stone.

Five ages

Advance your civilisation. Every age costs resources and time, and changes what you can build.

A real tech tree

27 technologies across seven buildings, from better axes to plate mail.

23 buildings

Houses, fields, barracks, docks and castles, with upgrades, and walls whose gates open for your own troops.

Command an army

Four stances, four formations, siege engines to pack and unpack, and five control groups under your thumb.

The dragon

She has a castle's hit points, flies over any terrain and breathes fire. Kill the mother, hold her nest, and her hatchling grows into yours.

Maps

Six map types, 2 to 8 players

Every skirmish can be a freshly generated map. The player count sets the size: a duel is 96×96 tiles, an eight-player war is 192×192. Fog of war hides everything you haven't scouted.

Island

One landmass with a beach and open sea all around. Palms on the shore.

River

A river cuts the land in two. Whoever holds the crossings holds the map.

Desert

Open sand, sparse dead wood and little cover. Every tree is worth fighting over.

Forest

Dense woodland between the players. Plenty of wood, and plenty of places to hide.

Archipelago

Each player starts on an island of their own, with deep water between.

Random

Let the generator pick. This one is an Island for eight players at 192×192.

Hand-drawn maps, plateaus and all

Besides the generator there is the MapMaker, a separate editor for drawing maps by hand: terrain, rivers, forests, starting positions, and raised plateaus ringed by cliffs that no unit can walk through.

Want to see what cliffs and plateaus look like? Download the PlatoSample map from Download More in the game. It is fully playable, but it's there to show the terrain off.

  • Five biomes, each with its own trees
  • Rivers with fords and bridges
  • Plateaus and cliff walls that block movement
  • Gold and stone veins near every start
  • One dragon nest per map
  • Save any match's map and play it again
Single player

Learn it, then beat the AI

Start with the How To Play campaign: five short scenarios that take you from your first villager to a real duel.

  1. Gather and build
  2. Climb the ages
  3. Find your opponent
  4. Tame a dragon
  5. Survive a real duel across one ford

Then open a skirmish against AI opponents on any map type and any win condition. There are five difficulties. A harder AI mostly reacts faster; only Unfair also starts with extra units:

Passive Easy Normal Hard Unfair

Save a game from the pause menu and pick it up later.

Multiplayer

Up to eight players on your network

One player hosts and everyone else joins. Games on your local network show up in the server browser by themselves: no IP addresses, no setup. Fill empty seats with AI.

Conquest

The classic rule. Lose every unit and building and you're out; the last player standing wins.

Trophy

Every player starts with a baby dragon. Lose yours and you're out. Guard it, or hunt theirs.

King of the Hill

Stand in the zone to score, and hold it alone to score three times as fast. First to the target wins.

Host

Pick the map type, size, win condition and starting age, then open the lobby. Seats, colours and teams are set there.

Join

Open Multiplayer. Games on your Wi-Fi are listed with their map, size, mode and free seats. Tap one to join.

One thing to know: the host's device runs the match. If the host drops out, the match ends. Playing over the internet (a master server and browser) is not built yet; today multiplayer means the same local network.

Content

Campaigns, maps and art packs

The download is kept small: art, campaigns and maps arrive as packs, fetched by the game itself. Packs marked Included download on first launch. Optional ones, including community content, are under Download More on the campaign screen.

Share your own map or campaign

Made something in the MapMaker? Community maps and campaigns can be published here as optional content, and every player can then download them in the game.

  1. Open a new issue on GitHub and start the title with #content.
  2. Say what it is (map or campaign), how many players, and what makes it worth playing.
  3. Attach the map folder (map.json and map.png) or the campaign folder as a zip.
  4. Confirm it is your own work and you're happy for it to be shared under CC BY-SA 3.0, the same licence as the game's art.

What gets accepted

  • It loads and can be played from start to finish
  • Starting positions are fair for every seat
  • Title and description say what it is
  • Nothing copied from somewhere it isn't allowed to be

Accepted content is packed and published as an optional download. An update to it is a new version, and players who have it are offered the update.

Download

Play the beta

Free and open source. The latest builds are on the GitHub releases page.

Latest release

Android

Download the .apk on your phone and open it. Android will ask you to allow installs from your browser or file manager. Landscape only.

Windows

Download the .exe and run it. Nothing to install. Windows may warn about an unrecognised app; choose More info → Run anyway.

On first launch the game downloads its art, about 80 MB. Player colours are an optional 227 MB extra; without them every army shows in its base colours. Both downloads resume if the connection drops.

This is a beta: playable and played, but not finished. Want the source? Build it yourself from GitHub with Godot 4.7.1.

Report a bug

Found something broken?

Tell us on GitHub. Start the issue title with #Bug so it's picked up.

A good report says:

  • which version you downloaded, and which phone or Windows version you play on
  • what you did, step by step
  • what you expected, and what happened instead
  • a screenshot, if it's something you can see

Ideas and questions

Not a bug? Suggestions and questions are welcome on the same issue tracker. Just leave the #Bug off the title.
