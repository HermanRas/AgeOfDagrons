Credits

Age of Dragon is an open-source project. This page records every third-party contribution used in the game. It is also shown in the game on the Credits screen, and it's a licence obligation, not a courtesy.

Per-asset provenance is tracked in game/assets/LICENCES.md and checked by tools/licence_audit.py. The full text is CREDITS.md in the repository.

Used for: unit, building, terrain and prop sprites, rendered from Wildfire Games' 3D models to 2D isometric sprite sheets; and sound effects and music.

The per-asset table (our ID, the recipe, and the exact 0 A.D. actor each came from) is game/assets/LICENCES.md, generated from the bake recipes so it cannot drift from what is actually baked.

App icon, boot splash and the how-to pages: AI-generated

Used for: the application launcher icon, the boot splash, the UI chrome and icon sets, and the six How To Play pages.

Origin: generated by the project owner using Google Gemini (paid account).

One caveat on the how-to pages: they are screen captures of this game with instructions painted over them, so the layer under the annotation is this project's rendering of 0 A.D. art and is covered by the CC-BY-SA 3.0 attribution above.

Licence: project assets, not third-party material; nothing here is owed to anyone else. Recorded because how an asset was made is part of its provenance, and because purely AI-generated images may not be copyrightable in some jurisdictions. Google's Terms of Service (effective 30 July 2026) state that for content its services generate, “Google won't claim ownership over that content”; commercial use is permitted on every tier. Under US law (Copyright Office guidance, 88 Fed. Reg. 16,190) purely AI-generated material is not registrable, so these files are ours to ship and probably not ours to restrict. That is why they are not described as MIT project code. Details: GeminiCopyRight.md.

Adding an entry

Any new third-party asset, addon or code contribution must be added to CREDITS.md in the same change that introduces it, together with game/assets/LICENCES.md. Credit only what is actually used, and only use licences compatible with an open-source CC-BY-SA art release.

Licence summary. Code is MIT. Art and audio are CC-BY-SA 3.0, because the sprites are rendered from 0 A.D.'s share-alike models. Reusing the art means attributing Wildfire Games as above.
